Welcome to my website!

My name is Ziyao Qing. I am a Dean's Scholar in the Bachelor of Pharmaceutical Science Advanced (Honours) Scholars Program at the Monash Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences (MIPS), Monash University, Melbourne.

I am passionate about understanding why the ageing brain becomes vulnerable to dementia, and about turning that knowledge into new medicines. My interests sit at the interface of neuroscience and pharmacology, with a particular focus on the glymphatic system, neuroinflammation and tau pathology.

Dementia affects more than 55 million people worldwide, yet treatments that truly change the course of the disease remain scarce. I believe that understanding how the brain's clearance systems, immune responses and cellular organelles fail with age will reveal new routes to therapies that can slow or prevent neurodegeneration.

To pursue this, I have trained in four research laboratories across Japan, each tackling neurodegeneration from a different angle – from the Alzheimer's-related kinase MARK4 and ageing-induced inflammation, to communication between organelles and the contribution of blood stem cells to dementia.
